Predicting liquidation cascades on Hyperliquid

Research with Prof. Ruizhe Jia, Stanford · 2026

Built a catalogue of 726 liquidation-cascade events across 84 perpetual-futures markets from ~1.8B raw fills, and showed that common cascade definitions barely agree with each other. Order-book features predict next-24h cascades with ROC-AUC above 0.9 within a market. Data engineering on a multi-TB on-chain dataset on the Stanford SC cluster.
